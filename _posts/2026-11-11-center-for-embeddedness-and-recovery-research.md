**A new name**
CEM Research Lab is now the Center for Embeddedness and Recovery Research. The new name reflects both halves of the work: the Criminogenic Embeddedness Model (CEM) for offending, and the Recovery Embeddedness Model (REM) for substance-use relapse and recovery. The center is independent and self-funded, and is not affiliated with a university. The web address, email, and archived citations stay the same.

**Where things stand**
Three manuscripts are under review at journals, and two book proposals are under consideration: a CEM theory monograph at Springer Nature and the REM book at West Virginia University Press. The first REM tests were preregistered on OSF in October 2026; the registration becomes public at the end of the year, and the results will be reported in the REM book. Analysis code for each study is archived on Zenodo. See [Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) and the updated [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}).
