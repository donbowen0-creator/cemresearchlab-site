<p class="post-meta">Donald Bowen, DBA, MS · Director, Center for Embeddedness and Recovery Research · Oceana, West Virginia · Updated November 2026</p>

## Profile

Criminologist and practicing behavioral health clinician with a DBA in criminal justice and master's degrees in criminal justice and sociology. Developer of the **Criminogenic Embeddedness Model (CEM)**, a life-course theory of offending, and its companion **Recovery Embeddedness Model (REM)** for substance-use relapse and recovery. The research program, begun in 2023, includes a published theory book, three preprints, three manuscripts under journal review, two book proposals under consideration, and openly archived analysis code. I bring 29 years in institutional and community corrections, 19 of them as a supervisor, and two current clinical caseloads in mental health and substance-use treatment in southern West Virginia.

## Research interests

Life-course and developmental criminology; desistance, reentry, and recidivism; substance-use recovery and relapse; corrections and alternatives to incarceration; poverty, unemployment, and justice outcomes; police accountability; rural and Appalachian justice and behavioral health.

## Methods and skills

- **Research and modeling:** Secondary analysis of national longitudinal cohorts (Add Health, NLSY97, Pathways to Desistance); logistic, linear probability, and event-history models; survey weights and cluster-robust estimation; OSF preregistration; reproducible R workflows archived on GitHub and Zenodo.
- **Grants and administration:** Grant writing, program budgeting, and board and county commission reporting.
- **Clinical intervention:** DSM-5-TR assessment and treatment planning; motivational interviewing; CBT, ACT, and DBT; group counseling; crisis intervention and de-escalation; telehealth.
- **Technology:** R/RStudio; Zoom and telehealth platforms; Google Workspace; Microsoft Office.
