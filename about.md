**Dr. Donald Bowen** is an independent researcher based in Oceana, West Virginia, and the developer of the Criminogenic Embeddedness Model (CEM).

His research grows out of 29 years in corrections, across both penal and community settings, including 19 years as a supervisor in a correctional facility and management of the McDowell County alternative sentencing program. He now works as a substance abuse and mental health counselor, and is pursuing graduate study in mental health counseling at Walden University.

## Education

- **DBA, Criminal Justice**, Northcentral University (now part of National University)
- **MS, Sociology**, Grand Canyon University

## Research interests

- Life-course and developmental criminology
- Integrating strain, control, social learning, and network theories
- Adult-onset offending and desistance
- Substance use relapse and recovery
