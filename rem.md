The Recovery Embeddedness Model (REM) asks whether the embeddedness logic of [CEM]({{ '/cem/' | relative_url }}) can help explain **substance use relapse and recovery**.

## Where it comes from

CEM proposes that embeddedness works asymmetrically: ties that pull a person toward offending form more easily than they come undone, and what remains of them can predict a return to offending later. That same pattern shows up often in substance use recovery work. REM tests whether the mechanism carries over from offending to relapse.

## Current status

REM is being tested against real longitudinal data before the full theory is built out. Results will be posted here and on the [blog]({{ '/blog/' | relative_url }}) as they become available.
